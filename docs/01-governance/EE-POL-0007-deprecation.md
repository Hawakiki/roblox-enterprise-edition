# Deprecation Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy will set how documents, rules and code are deprecated, and how long they are kept before they are removed.

## 2. Status

This number is reserved.
The policy will be drafted when something is deprecated for the first time, through a change request of its own.
Until then it states no rules, and nothing may be done or forbidden under its authority.
