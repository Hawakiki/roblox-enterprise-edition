# Delegation of Authority

## 1. Purpose

This policy will set which decisions each position may approve on its own authority, and which must go higher.

## 2. Status

This number is reserved.
The policy will be drafted together with the organization chart, through a change request of its own.
Until then it states no rules, and nothing may be done or forbidden under its authority.
