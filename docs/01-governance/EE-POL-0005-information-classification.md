# Information Classification Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy will take over the classifications now set out in EE-POL-0001 §12, and set who may see what.

## 2. Status

This number is reserved.
The policy will be drafted when the Company holds information worth classifying, through a change request of its own.
Until then it states no rules, and nothing may be done or forbidden under its authority.
