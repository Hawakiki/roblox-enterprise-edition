# Enterprise Versioning Standard

## 1. Purpose

This standard will set how releases of EnterpriseEdition are numbered and tagged.

## 2. Status

This number is reserved.
The standard will be drafted together with the first release, through a change request of its own.
Until then it states no rules, and nothing may be done or forbidden under its authority.
