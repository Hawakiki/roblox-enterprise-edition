# Charter of EnterpriseEdition

## Article 1. Name

The organization is named EnterpriseEdition, hereinafter "the Company".

## Article 2. Purpose

The Company exists to deliver value.
Value is delivered as output, output through a service, and the service through the proper channels.
Delivering value through improper channels, including directly, is not delivering value.

## Article 3. Core Values

1. **Trust the Registry, not the Memory.**
   What is not written down did not happen.
   What is written down but not registered does not exist.
2. **Process over Outcome.**
   A correct outcome reached by an incorrect process is an incident.
3. **Every Change Has a Request.**
   This includes the change that introduced requests, and this Charter.
4. **Two Languages, One Truth.**
   Every document exists in English and in Korean.
   The English version is the truth (EE-POL-0002).
5. **Indirection Is Care.**
   Nothing that can be delivered through an abstraction shall be delivered without one.

## Article 4. Authority of Documents

1. This Charter is the root of the document hierarchy.
   Every controlled document derives its authority from this Charter, directly or through its parent (EE-POL-0001).
2. Where documents conflict, the document at the higher level prevails.
3. A rule that is not written in a controlled document is not a rule.
4. A rule that is written in a controlled document but not enforced is a recommendation.

## Article 5. Governing Bodies

1. The Board of Directors is the highest decision-making body of the Company.
2. Until the Board of Directors is constituted, the Approver of record acts on its behalf.
   The Approver of record is the person named as Approver in the Approval section of this Charter.

## Article 6. Amendment

This Charter is amended only through a change request approved by the Board of Directors.

## Article 7. Effect

1. This Charter takes effect on approval.
2. Acts performed before this Charter took effect remain valid and shall not be rewritten.
   This applies in particular to commit `8f2b559`, which predates every rule of the Company.
